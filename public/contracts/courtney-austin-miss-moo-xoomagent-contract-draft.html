1. Scope of Services
1.1XoomAI will deploy, configure, train and manage a dedicated eight-pillar XoomAgent™ platform ("the System") for the Client, comprising:
  1. Orchestration — Coordinate approved work across the platform, maintain task context, route exceptions to the correct Miss Moo manager and preserve human approval controls.
  2. Participant and Customer Success — Support participant, family and referrer enquiry triage, follow-up, complaint or issue escalation and consistent communications without replacing direct human relationships or care decisions.
  3. Growth — Capture approved website and referral enquiries, maintain lead and referral records, prepare follow-ups and support pipeline visibility.
  4. Finance — Support approved Astalty-to-Xero workflows, invoice and shift exception checks, reconciliation preparation and management reporting. Final payroll, invoice, payment and financial approvals remain with authorised Client personnel.
  5. Automation — Build and maintain approved workflows for staff availability, roster preparation, shift validation, staff onboarding, document follow-up, record updates and cross-system tasks.
  6. HR & People — Support employee onboarding, employment-document collection, identity and compliance-document tracking, signature reminders and onboarding status reporting. Employment and compliance decisions remain with the Client.
  7. Media Hub — Build an agreed brand system, prepare platform-specific content from Client-approved imagery, route content for approval and schedule approved posts to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Paid advertising is only activated by written approval.
  8. Data & Analytics — Analyse approved operational data, shift notes and incident reports for missing information, recurring themes and emerging risks, then prepare structured summaries for human review.
  9. Executive Virtual Assistants — Provide up to five configured executive virtual assistants for approved scheduling, communications, document, follow-up and operational support workflows.
1.2The initial implementation will prioritise: (a) Astalty staff-availability collection and roster preparation; (b) rules-based validation of completed shifts and automatic approval only where Client-approved criteria are satisfied; (c) human exception queues for unmatched times, missing shift notes, complex roster changes, SIL-house requirements and other identified risks; (d) employee onboarding and compliance-document workflows; and (e) shift-note and incident-report analysis for manager review.
1.3The Services cover Miss Moo Disability Support Pty Ltd as a single deployment and are limited to the scope above unless both parties approve a written variation.
1.4XoomAI will integrate the System with the Client's approved Astalty, Xero, Microsoft 365, document storage, CRM and social-media systems as reasonably required, subject to available APIs, account permissions, licensing and third-party platform limits.
1.5The Client must review and approve workflow rules, staffing and participant-matching criteria, escalation thresholds, data access, external communications and production actions before activation. Participant care, staff performance, payroll, finance, incident response and regulatory decisions always remain subject to authorised human judgement.
2. Platform & Integrations
2.1XoomAI will deploy the System in an isolated Microsoft Azure cloud environment and connect it to the agreed Client systems. No on-premise hardware is included.
2.2The deployment will use reasonable security controls, access management, logging, backup and environment separation appropriate to the agreed scope.
2.3Microsoft Azure hosting is charged to the Client at actual cost and is estimated at $150 + GST per month. The amount may vary with usage, storage, backup, network and cloud-provider pricing.
2.4Astalty, Xero, Microsoft 365, CRM, telephony, advertising spend, phone or messaging usage, API consumption and other third-party platform fees are excluded unless expressly included in a written quotation or invoice. The connected social-publishing tool currently allows two social accounts; additional accounts are estimated at $6 + GST per account per month, subject to third-party pricing.
2.5Service availability remains subject to Microsoft Azure, Astalty, Xero, Microsoft 365, social platforms, third-party AI services, APIs, internet connectivity and scheduled maintenance.

4. Implementation Timeline
4.1XoomAI will deliver the System across the following phases, commencing upon receipt of the first invoice payment:
(a)Implementation scoping (Days 1–2): Conduct a 45-minute scoping session with Courtney and relevant operational leaders; confirm priority workflows, system owners, data categories, business rules and human approval points.
(b)Cloud deployment and connections (Days 1–5): Provision the isolated Azure environment and connect approved systems where access and suitable APIs are available.
(c)Priority workflow build and testing (Days 3–10): Configure, test and refine the agreed roster, shift-validation, onboarding and document-analysis workflows using Client-approved scenarios and rules.
(d)Controlled go-live (Target: end of Week 2): Train initial users, activate approved workflows with human review and monitor exceptions. Remaining pillars and workflows will be introduced iteratively in the agreed priority order.
4.2The target for the initial controlled deployment is approximately two weeks. This is a target, not a guaranteed completion date for every capability. Delays caused by the Client, third-party access, API limitations, licensing, security review or feedback may extend the timeline.

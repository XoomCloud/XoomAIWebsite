1. Agreement structure

This Agreement consists of these core terms and Schedule 1 (Service Scope). If the documents conflict, the order of priority is: any signed variation; Schedule 1; then these core terms.

2. Fees

Onboarding fee$3,000 + GST once-offDiscovery, solution design, configuration and initial deployment.
  • All amounts are in Australian dollars and exclude GST.
  • Invoice timing, payment method, due dates, included usage allowances and any third-party charges are to be confirmed before issue.
  • Work, usage, licences or integrations outside the agreed scope require written agreement and may incur additional fees.
  • XoomAI will not change the agreed fees except under a signed variation or an agreed review mechanism inserted before execution.

3. Onboarding and delivery

During onboarding, the parties will confirm systems, data sources, workflows, access permissions, authorised users, escalation rules, approval points, operating hours, channel usage, acceptance criteria and deployment priorities. Delivery dates depend on timely customer access, decisions and third-party availability.

The onboarding fee covers the initial implementation of the agreed service scope. Material rework caused by a customer scope change, inaccessible systems or new requirements is subject to a written variation.

4. Customer responsibilities

Masters Empire Group will provide accurate instructions and lawful access to required systems and data; nominate authorised contacts; maintain required third-party licences; review outputs and exceptions promptly; and ensure appropriately qualified people retain decisions that require professional judgement.

5. Human control and regulated activities

The AI Employees assist authorised personnel; they do not replace accountable professional judgement.
  • No AI Employee may give legal advice, make a credit decision, approve a loan, make a regulatory determination, execute a binding customer commitment, or represent that a compliance obligation has been satisfied.
  • Legal, lending, privacy, compliance, settlement, customer and commercial decisions remain with Masters Empire Group's authorised and appropriately qualified people.
  • External communications, commitments and consequential actions will use the approval controls agreed during onboarding.

6. Data, privacy and security

Each party must comply with applicable privacy and data-protection obligations relevant to its activities. Masters Empire Group is responsible for having lawful authority to provide personal information and system access. XoomAI will use customer data only to provide and support the services, apply reasonable access controls, and notify the customer of a confirmed material security incident affecting customer data without undue delay.

Specific hosting regions, retention periods, subprocessors, security controls and deletion requirements are to be documented during onboarding or before production access.

7. Intellectual property

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, data, systems, methods and intellectual property. Masters Empire Group retains ownership of its customer data and business materials. XoomAI retains ownership of its platform, reusable tools, templates, orchestration, methods and general improvements. Subject to payment, Masters Empire Group receives a non-exclusive right to use customer-specific deliverables for its internal business purposes during the service term.

8. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other's confidential information, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to people who need it and are bound to protect it. This does not apply to information that is public through no breach, independently developed, already lawfully known, or required to be disclosed by law.

9. Service management and changes

XoomAI will monitor and maintain the managed service and address faults within the support arrangements agreed in Schedule 1. Either party may request a change. A material change to scope, fees, risk, integrations or delivery timing must be recorded in a written variation approved by both parties.

10. Warranties and outcomes

Each party warrants that it has authority to enter this Agreement. XoomAI will provide the services with due care and skill. AI and third-party systems may produce errors or become unavailable; therefore, the customer must maintain the agreed review and approval controls. No revenue, savings, accuracy, funding, approval, legal, compliance or settlement outcome is guaranteed.

11. Liability

Nothing in this Agreement excludes rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Subject to that, the final liability cap, excluded loss categories, indemnities and insurance requirements are to be reviewed and approved before issue. Neither party should rely on this draft as containing agreed liability terms.

12. Suspension and termination

The parties must insert the agreed initial term, renewal, notice period, rights to remedy breach, suspension rights, payment consequences and transition assistance before signing. XoomAI may suspend access where reasonably necessary to address a material security risk, unlawful use or overdue undisputed fees, after notice where practicable.

13. General

The parties must confirm the governing law and courts before issue. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except as part of a genuine corporate restructure or sale of substantially all relevant business assets. Notices, entire-agreement, waiver, severability, force-majeure and electronic-signature provisions will apply in the final approved version.

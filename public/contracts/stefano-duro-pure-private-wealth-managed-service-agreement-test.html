Security Focused

Securing your business with Xoom Cloud to the Essential 8 Framework.

In today's digital age, protecting your business from cyber threats is more critical than ever. The Essential 8 framework provides robust strategies to mitigate cybersecurity risks. By leveraging our solutions, you can ensure your business is well-protected across all key areas of the framework.

  1. Application Whitelisting Implementing application whitelisting ensures that only approved applications can run on your systems. This prevents unauthorized software from executing, reducing the risk of malware infections and other malicious activities.

  2. Patch Applications Regularly updating and patching applications is crucial to protect against known vulnerabilities. Automated patch management solutions can streamline this process, ensuring all software is up-to-date and secure.

  3. Configure Microsoft Office Macro Settings Restricting the use of macros in Microsoft Office applications can prevent the execution of malicious code. By configuring macro settings only to allow trusted macros, you can reduce the risk of macro-based attacks.

  4. User Application Hardening user applications involves disabling or restricting features that are not necessary for business operations. This includes disabling Flash, ads, and Java, which are common attack vectors.

  5. Restrict Administrative Privileges Limiting administrative privileges to only those who need them reduces the risk of unauthorized access and potential damage. Implementing role-based access control and regularly reviewing user permissions are essential steps.

  6. Patch Operating Systems: Keeping operating systems up-to-date with the latest patches is vital for maintaining security. Automated patch management solutions can ensure that all systems are regularly updated, minimizing the risk of exploitation.

  7. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Implementing MFA adds an extra layer of security by requiring users to provide multiple verification forms before accessing sensitive systems. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if passwords are compromised.

  8. Daily Backups: Regularly backing up data ensures you can quickly recover during a cyber incident. Automated backup solutions can streamline this process, ensuring that all critical data is securely backed up and easily recoverable.

By integrating Xoom Cloud into your business operations, you can quickly start to achieve compliance in the Essential 8 framework and enhance your overall security posture. These measures protect your business from cyber threats and ensure compliance with industry standards and best practices.

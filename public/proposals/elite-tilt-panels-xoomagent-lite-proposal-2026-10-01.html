Proposed digital staff

Four roles aligned to the work

Office Administrator

Supports the daily administrative flow and keeps agreed records, communications and schedules organised.

  • Triage shared inboxes and prepare routine responses
  • Coordinate appointments, calendars and reminders
  • Prepare documents and maintain agreed registers
  • Follow up missing information and escalate exceptions

Operations Coordinator

Coordinates approved work across people, suppliers, schedules and job systems.

  • Capture and validate new operational requests
  • Maintain job status and next-action visibility
  • Coordinate routine scheduling and stakeholder updates
  • Escalate delays, conflicts and missing dependencies

Estimator / Quoting Officer

Prepares structured quote inputs and controlled drafts for an authorised estimator or manager to approve.

  • Capture enquiry details, plans and specifications
  • Extract scope, quantities and missing-information questions
  • Prepare quote packs using approved templates and rates
  • Track quote status and coordinate approved follow-up

Project Administrator

Keeps approved projects organised from handover through delivery and close-out.

  • Create and maintain project folders and records
  • Track documentation, milestones and action items
  • Prepare routine project updates and meeting follow-ups
  • Coordinate close-out documents and outstanding items

Two Executive VAs are included at no additional cost

XoomAgent™ Lite also includes two Executive Virtual Assistants for Mike and a second nominated executive. These personal support roles sit outside the four configured staff roles above and can assist with approved email, calendar, meeting, follow-up and organisational tasks.

Delivery approach

Scope first. Automate in controlled stages.

1. Implementation scoping

Confirm the first workflows, source documents, connected systems, responsible people and approval boundaries.

2. Configure roles and knowledge

Set up the four digital staff roles, two included Executive VAs, permissions, knowledge sources and agreed integrations.

3. Validate with real scenarios

Test inputs, outputs, escalation paths and human review using controlled Elite Tilt Panels examples.

4. Launch progressively

Release approved workflows in stages, monitor performance and refine them using operating feedback.

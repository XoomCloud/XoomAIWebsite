Proposed solution

One Orchestrator coordinating three specialist functions around Jerry's immediate priority: marketing, lead management and sales performance.

Orchestrator

Keeps the specialist functions aligned and gives Jerry one place to direct priorities.

  • Receives campaign, content and sales instructions in plain language
  • Routes work to the right specialist function
  • Maintains approval points for external actions
  • Produces concise briefs, exceptions and next-action summaries

Advertising and organic content

Runs the repetitive production and optimisation work across paid and organic channels.

  • Prepare Meta campaign structure, copy, artwork and landing-page recommendations
  • Monitor results and recommend or apply approved budget and creative changes
  • Run controlled creative testing to prevent campaign fatigue
  • Create, adapt, schedule and publish on-brand organic posts across agreed social channels

Sales-pipeline management

Protects Jerry's time by responding quickly and separating genuine prospects from low-intent enquiries.

  • Capture leads in HubSpot and maintain pipeline status and next actions
  • Ask Jerry's approved qualification questions
  • Run consistent follow-up and controlled nurture sequences
  • Book qualified prospects into Jerry's calendar for the advisory conversation

Performance and market analytics

Connects advertising activity to pipeline outcomes and useful property-market insight.

  • Report spend, reach, clicks, leads, qualified opportunities and booked meetings
  • Identify weak campaigns, strong audiences and creative fatigue
  • Compare marketing activity with sales-pipeline movement
  • Prepare approved Victorian and Australian property-market content inputs

Priority workflows

1

Campaign creation to continuous optimisation

XoomAgent™ prepares campaign structure, audience, copy, artwork and landing-page inputs, then monitors performance and tests approved changes. Meta is the initial focus; Google Ads can be added where the economics support it.

2

Lead captured to qualified appointment

New enquiries flow into HubSpot, receive an immediate response and move through Jerry's approved qualification process. Credible prospects are booked; time-wasters are filtered or placed into a controlled nurture path.

3

Market idea to multi-channel content

Jerry can provide a topic such as the Victorian property market. XoomAgent™ turns it into on-brand social content, adapts the wording and creative for each agreed platform, and schedules the approved posts.

4

Weekly performance brief

Campaign and HubSpot data are combined into a direct report showing spend, enquiries, qualified leads, booked appointments, pipeline value, strengths, risks and recommended actions.

Focused scope, clear boundary

This starter deployment is limited to advertising, organic social content, sales-pipeline management and performance analytics. It does not include the full operations, finance, HR, property-repository or client-service workforce proposed previously. Those functions can be added later if the business case changes.

Our understanding

Win on responsiveness. Keep your technical standards.

You described two solar businesses, an increasingly competitive market and expensive technical labour. Your immediate need is for new enquiries to receive a prompt response, stay engaged until a person can help, and leave a clear record in the CRM. You also want marketing, quoting, installation administration, invoicing and customer service to work together rather than buying disconnected tools.

Your existing systems

You confirmed Pylon for solar design and both Xero and MYOB for finance. HubSpot was discussed as the sales CRM, alongside an existing paid subscription you were not using. We will confirm the right account and entity mapping rather than assume a new licence is needed.

A managed service, not a DIY install

XoomAI configures the employees and agreed workflows, manages platform LLM usage, support and updates, and trains your team. Technical advice, commitments and sensitive actions remain with authorised people.

Recommended Lite team · four employees

A focused commercial front end.

If the lower monthly commitment is the priority, these four roles address the clearest immediate needs: response speed, marketing, quotation preparation and repetitive administration. Lite includes 4 AI Employees plus 2 Executive VAs. It does not include AirCall or the inbound AI Receptionist; project delivery, specialist receivables and compliance administration remain with your team.

1 · Sales Representative

Capture Meta and website leads, ask approved questions about postcode, property, solar/battery/EV needs and timing, keep the enquiry engaged by text or email, and update HubSpot with the conversation and next step before a human takes over.

2 · Marketing Coordinator

Draft technically accurate solar, battery and EV charging content; prepare Meta campaign and funnel assets; coordinate approved publication and report on campaign-to-enquiry performance. Ad spend and publishing decisions remain with A One.

3 · Estimator / Quoting Officer

Collect electricity bills and project requirements, organise Pylon design inputs, apply only A One’s approved rebate and calculation rules, and draft quotations and follow-ups. A qualified person approves system design, savings statements, pricing and issue.

4 · Office Administrator

Triage shared enquiries, validate customer details, chase missing documents and organise quote/job records. Keep the hand-off to the sales and technical team visible rather than letting leads disappear between inboxes.

Prepare customer and invoice records in the agreed Xero or MYOB entity, follow approved deposit/progress/final-payment rules, track due dates and respectful reminders, and escalate disputes. Confirm the applicable 20%/progressive or 50%/50% arrangement per job.

Support the included AirCall inbound AI Receptionist: greet callers, disclose AI handling, capture and qualify enquiries, answer from approved FAQs and route to the right person. Handle installation-status and handover questions, with technical and urgent safety issues escalated.

8 · IT Support Officer

Triage staff IT requests, guide troubleshooting using approved instructions, assist with access requests and onboarding checklists, and record issues for escalation to your authorised IT provider. Priority workflows

Build a connected process in controlled stages.

1 · Enquiry → qualified opportunity

Capture the agreed lead source, send an approved acknowledgement, collect project details and update the CRM. Keep the customer engaged and alert a human with the complete context. Full also captures and routes inbound calls through AirCall and the AI Receptionist.

2 · Bill → Pylon inputs → reviewed quote

Gather bills and property requirements, prepare approved calculations and Pylon project inputs, assemble a quotation draft and track follow-up. Pylon API permissions and supported design actions must be tested; autonomous CAD design is not promised.

3 · Accepted quote → installation readiness

Full coordinates the approved hand-off into Pylon, job pack, application checklist, site inspection and crew schedule. Required approvals and missing documentation are flagged before the relevant milestone. Lite retains these delivery steps as human-led work.

4 · Milestones → invoices → handover

Full prepares approved deposit, progress and final invoice actions in the correct Xero/MYOB entity and tracks handover evidence and customer updates. No payments, rebate eligibility decisions or electrical certification are delegated without human authority.

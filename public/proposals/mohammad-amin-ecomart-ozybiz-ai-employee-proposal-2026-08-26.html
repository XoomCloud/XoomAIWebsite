Priority workflows

The first connected journeys to build

1. Enquiry to approved quote

Capture the customer and need from phone, email or web; identify relevant products; retrieve the authorised price; prepare a quote; obtain any required approval; send it; schedule follow-up; and keep HubSpot current.

2. Approved order to dispatch

Validate the order details, create the warehouse task, notify the correct operator, record pick-and-pack progress, coordinate courier or 3PL handoff and proactively communicate exceptions.

3. Product data to Shopify

Validate the source file, flag missing attributes, prepare product copy and imagery, route price and claim changes for approval, publish accepted updates and report failed or incomplete items.

4. Stock and catalogue exception control

Compare approved product information with the available stock and catalogue context, surface missing attributes or discrepancies and route each exception to the authorised person instead of guessing or publishing an unsupported change.

5. Content plan to qualified demand

Build an approved weekly content rhythm, create assets and copy for each business, publish only after the applicable review, capture responses and route serious enquiries into the sales workflow.

6. Daily operational control

Deliver one concise brief showing new enquiries, quotes awaiting approval, orders awaiting action, dispatch risks, supplier exceptions, campaign activity, owner and due time.

Delivery plan

Phase the rollout around evidence and control

Phase 1 — Discovery and data readiness

Confirm legal entities, brands, teams, operating locations, customer channels, product sources, customer-specific pricing, stock ownership, 3PL arrangements, HubSpot, Shopify, Xero and approval rights.

Phase 2 — Foundation and shared queues

Configure the four employees, permissions, approved knowledge, task queues, audit history and daily operational brief. Establish separate brand and data rules for EcoMart and OzyBiz.

Phase 3 — Customer service and quotation pilot

Release a limited enquiry and quote-preparation workflow with human approval, test customer-specific prices and monitor unanswered questions, escalations and CRM accuracy.

Phase 4 — Order and dispatch pilot

Connect approved orders to local warehouse or 3PL tasks, validate pick-and-pack handoffs, test courier updates and measure exception visibility.

Phase 5 — Shopify and marketing

Clean priority catalogue data, implement controlled bulk updates and launch an approved cross-channel content and nurture schedule.

Phase 6 — Review the next catalogue roles

Use operating evidence to decide whether the next employee should be the Procurement Officer, AI Dispatch Coordinator, AI Shipment Tracking Officer, AI Inventory Coordinator or another defined role from the selector. Refine the initial four monthly as volume and complexity grow.

Reasonable questions

What Mohammad may want clarified

“Can it use our customer-specific prices?”

Yes, where the approved price source and customer matching rules are reliable. We will test representative accounts and require human review where a price is missing, conflicting, unusual or commercially sensitive.

“Can it update Shopify products?”

Subject to authorised Shopify access, it can prepare and perform controlled bulk updates. Price changes, product claims and major catalogue changes will use agreed approval rules.

“Can it perform actions in Xero?”

Yes. XoomAI can supply AI Employees whose defined roles connect to Xero and perform approved actions. The exact work depends on the employees selected, Xero access, data quality and the controls agreed during implementation; payment release and other consequential financial decisions remain with authorised people.

“Can it coordinate our warehouse and 3PL?”

Yes, when each handoff, status source and exception path is defined. Direct system actions depend on provider access; otherwise XoomAgent can manage a controlled task and notification workflow.

“Will it replace the offshore team?”

The first objective is to remove repeated coordination and make ownership visible. Mohammad can then decide which tasks remain human, which become AI-assisted and which can be automated after evidence from the pilot.

“Can we change the four roles?”

Yes. This proposal recommends four exact catalogue roles based on the call. Mohammad can use the Employee Selector to compare all 60 defined positions and agree a substitution with XoomAI before implementation.

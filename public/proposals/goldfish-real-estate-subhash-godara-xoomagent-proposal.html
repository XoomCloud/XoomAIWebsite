The outcome

Goldfish gets one managed operating layer that handles routine enquiries quickly, prepares the right follow-up, keeps records current and gives the team control over every material client-facing action.

Goldfish publicly supports property buying, selling and leasing and operates across multiple property categories. During the strategy session, Subhash identified a 16–17 person team and a clear operational drain: staff time spent answering basic calls, sending template emails, arranging follow-ups and booking appraisal appointments. XoomAgent™ starts with those high-frequency tasks, then expands into compliant CRM and property-data workflows.

Response

Handle routine enquiries sooner

Answer common questions from approved business information, identify the caller or enquirer where records allow, and route complex or sensitive matters to the right person.

Capacity

Remove repeat administration

Prepare template responses, record messages, schedule approved appointments and create follow-up tasks without asking staff to re-key the same information.

Control

Protect reputation and data

Use role-based permissions, human approval gates, audit trails and dedicated infrastructure so automation does not become an uncontrolled client touchpoint.

Ten practical workflow opportunities

These examples show where the Goldfish XoomAgent™ workforce can remove repetitive administration across sales, leasing and property management. The launch sequence will be scoped against Goldfish’s actual licences, permissions, processes and compliance obligations.

1

Calls, enquiries and appraisal appointments

  1. Receive routine phone, website chat and app enquiries and identify the person against approved CRM records where a reliable match exists.
  2. Answer common questions from a controlled Goldfish knowledge base covering services, offices, listings, inspections and standard process information.
  3. Capture the enquiry, property, urgency and preferred next step, then route it to the appropriate sales, leasing, property management or administration team.
  4. Draft or send low-risk template acknowledgements only where Goldfish has approved the template and rule; keep advice, negotiation and unusual requests behind human approval.
  5. Offer approved appraisal or follow-up appointment times, create the calendar event and write the outcome back to the nominated system.
2

Rex CRM follow-up and record hygiene

  1. Connect to Rex CRM through its approved API, native integration, webhook or authorised export/import method, subject to Goldfish’s Rex plan and permissions.
  2. Create or update contact, appraisal, enquiry and property records from approved calls, forms and inboxes while preventing duplicates.
  3. Prepare follow-up tasks and message drafts based on enquiry type, stage and elapsed time, with consent and human approval rules applied before external contact.
  4. Surface incomplete records, overdue follow-ups and unassigned enquiries in a practical queue for staff action.
  5. Maintain a traceable source and action history so the team can see what was changed, why it changed and who approved it.
3

RP Data/CoreLogic property intelligence

  1. Use licensed RP Data/CoreLogic APIs, supported integrations or authorised exports to retrieve the property and market fields Goldfish is entitled to use.
  2. Combine approved property intelligence with Rex CRM records to prepare appraisal research, comparable-property packs and internal follow-up context.
  3. Validate addresses, source dates and required fields, then flag missing or conflicting data rather than inventing a result.
  4. Prepare a structured appraisal or prospecting brief for staff review, with source attribution and no automatic claim or valuation sent to a client.
  5. Extend the same controlled pattern to PriceFinder, PropertyMe, Agentbox or other systems named during the call where their licences and supported integration methods permit it.
4

Open-home and inspection follow-up

  1. Import approved inspection registrations and attendance results from the nominated property or CRM system.
  2. Group attendees by interest, finance readiness, timing and requested follow-up without making an automated judgement about eligibility.
  3. Prepare approved thank-you messages, property information and next-step prompts for staff review or rules-based delivery.
  4. Create agent call tasks for high-intent buyers and place unanswered questions into a visible follow-up queue.
  5. Summarise buyer feedback for the listing agent and prepare a vendor-update draft with personal or sensitive details excluded.
5

Appraisal nurture and seller conversion

  1. Capture appraisal requests from calls, forms, referrals and campaigns and create a complete Rex CRM record.
  2. Check calendars, offer approved appointment windows and send confirmations and reminders.
  3. Prepare a pre-appraisal brief using authorised CRM history and licensed property data.
  4. Prompt the assigned agent when an appraisal has no recorded outcome or next action within Goldfish’s agreed timeframe.
  5. Draft a compliant, personalised nurture sequence for human approval based on the seller’s stated timing and preferences.
6

Buyer matching and new-listing alerts

  1. Maintain buyer requirements from approved conversations and forms, including location, budget, property type and timing.
  2. Compare new or updated listings against recorded requirements and produce a ranked review list for the sales team.
  3. Draft approved listing alerts for matched buyers while applying consent, frequency and unsubscribe rules.
  4. Record responses, inspection requests and changed requirements back into Rex CRM.
  5. Escalate finance, negotiation, legal or advice-related questions to the assigned agent rather than answering automatically.
7

Rental and maintenance request triage

  1. Receive tenant requests from approved inboxes, forms or phone channels and capture the property, issue, urgency and supporting files.
  2. Apply Goldfish’s documented triage rules to distinguish routine requests from potential emergencies, with immediate human escalation where required.
  3. Create the task in the approved property-management system and request missing information using controlled templates.
  4. Prepare owner, tenant and contractor updates for approval and track unanswered actions against service targets.
  5. Provide a daily exception summary covering urgent, overdue and blocked maintenance matters.
8

Listing launch and campaign coordination

  1. Create a launch checklist when a listing reaches the approved stage, with owners, due dates and required assets.
  2. Track photography, floorplans, copy, signboards, portal fields, social content and vendor approvals in one status view.
  3. Prepare listing copy and channel variations from verified property facts, with every public claim held for human review.
  4. Flag inconsistent prices, dates, features or missing disclaimers before publication.
  5. Record approval history and notify the responsible person when a dependency threatens the planned launch date.
9

Contract-to-settlement coordination

  1. Start an internal milestone checklist from an approved sale record without providing legal advice or interpreting contracts.
  2. Track finance, building and pest, deposit, unconditional and settlement dates supplied by authorised staff or systems.
  3. Prepare reminder drafts and internal tasks ahead of agreed milestones.
  4. Route missing documents, changed dates and exceptions to the responsible agent or administrator.
  5. Prepare a settlement-day handover checklist and post-settlement follow-up tasks for review.
10

Daily management dashboard and exception queue

  1. Combine approved activity data from calls, website enquiries, calendars, Rex CRM and connected property systems.
  2. Show new enquiries, response times, appraisal bookings, overdue follow-ups, inspection activity and unresolved exceptions.
  3. Highlight records that are unassigned, incomplete, duplicated or outside Goldfish’s agreed service targets.
  4. Produce role-specific daily summaries for agents, team leaders and management rather than sending every alert to everyone.
  5. Track workflow volume, completion, human overrides and estimated administration time returned to the team.

Explicit compliance boundary

This proposal excludes realestate.com.au scraping and any grey-area scraping, anti-bot bypass, browser circumvention or unauthorised data collection. XoomAI will use approved APIs, native integrations, authorised exports, webhooks, permitted email parsing and human approval gates only. If a vendor does not provide a compliant access path, that workflow will not be automated.

Pillar 4

Pillar 5

Automation

Connects approved phone, email, calendar, website, Rex CRM and RP Data/CoreLogic workflows, monitors exceptions and keeps repeat processes documented, testable and supportable.

Pillar 6

HR & People

Supports onboarding, training, policy knowledge, role handovers, recurring people administration and process consistency across Goldfish’s 16–17 person team.

Pillar 7

Media Hub

Prepares on-brand listing, service and market content for review, adapts approved material for each channel and maintains a visible approval queue before anything is published.

Pillar 8

Data & Analytics

Turns enquiry response times, appraisal activity, CRM follow-up, source performance and workflow exceptions into useful dashboards and management reporting.

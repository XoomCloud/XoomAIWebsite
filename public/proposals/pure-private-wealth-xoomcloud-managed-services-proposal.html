The outcome

Give Pure Private Wealth one managed layer for endpoint security, cloud-workspace protection, preventative maintenance and day-to-day support across four PCs and seven users.

Pure Private Wealth works with sensitive personal and financial information. The service is designed to reduce avoidable cyber risk, keep supported devices current and give the team a clear place to turn when something is not working. XoomCloud manages the protection and maintenance layer while Pure Private Wealth keeps control of business decisions and client relationships.

XoomCloud Business coverage

Security, maintenance and support managed as one service rather than a collection of disconnected tools.

Endpoint security

Managed AV and EDR/MDR

Managed antivirus, endpoint detection and response, and managed detection and response across the supported PCs to identify suspicious behaviour and support a faster response.

Threat protection

Malware and ransomware defence

Layered anti-malware and ransomware protection designed to reduce the risk of malicious files, unsafe activity and common endpoint-based attacks.

Email security

Anti-phishing protection

Protection against common phishing and credential-theft attempts, helping reduce the risk posed by deceptive email and malicious links.

Cloud workspace

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace

Protection for the agreed Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace environment across the seven covered users, configured to support the platform Pure Private Wealth operates.

Maintenance

Patch management

Managed operating-system and supported application patching to reduce exposure to known issues and keep the four supported PCs current.

Risk visibility

Vulnerability scanning

Ongoing scanning to identify relevant device vulnerabilities, prioritise remediation and give Pure Private Wealth clearer visibility of its security posture.

Support for the devices the team relies on

XoomCloud provides 9:00 am–5:00 pm business support for the four supported PCs. The detailed support process, response expectations, approved contacts and any exclusions will be documented in the service agreement before commencement.

How the managed service works

1

Confirm the environment

2

Deploy and configure protection

Install the agreed endpoint security tools, configure cloud-workspace protection and establish the required policies for patching, scanning and alert handling.

3

Establish support and monitoring

Confirm how users request help, document the supported environment and begin ongoing monitoring, maintenance and vulnerability review.

4

Maintain and improve

Keep supported devices current, review security findings, respond to relevant alerts and adjust the managed environment as Pure Private Wealth's needs change.

Optional virtual desktops for external staff

For external staff, the recommended model is a managed Windows 11 virtual desktop within Microsoft 365, keeping business access inside a controlled work environment.

Recommended approach

Microsoft-hosted Windows 11 desktop

Provide each external team member with a dedicated virtual PC rather than relying on an unmanaged personal device. Pure Private Wealth can purchase the virtual desktops directly from Microsoft.

Indicative Microsoft cost

Approximately $105 per virtual PC per month

Quantity is to be confirmed. This cost is separate from the XoomCloud Business investment, is paid directly to Microsoft and may vary with Microsoft pricing, configuration and licensing.

Why this helps

A controlled virtual desktop creates a clearer boundary between Pure Private Wealth data and an external staff member's personal device. It also supports more consistent access, security policy and offboarding. Final design depends on identity, application, data-location, performance and licensing requirements.

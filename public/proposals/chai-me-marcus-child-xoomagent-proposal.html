The outcome

Chai Me gets one supported operating layer that turns sales demand into an actionable production and procurement plan, then builds a repeatable B2B prospecting engine without another open-ended development project.

Chai Me manufactures drinking chocolate, chai, coffee syrups and non-alcoholic drinks for a predominantly B2B customer base. During the strategy session, Marcus identified two immediate bottlenecks consuming about 20 hours each week: manual production and procurement planning, and prospect research and outreach that falls well below the team’s intended volume.

The two workflows to tackle first

These are the two immediate priorities discussed at the start of the strategy session. Both will be validated against Chai Me’s live processes, licences and data before any automated action is enabled.

1

Unleashed production and procurement planning

  1. Collect approved sales-order and inventory data from Unleashed, including orders entered manually from email and orders received through the Shopify integration.
  2. Compare open demand, stock on hand, expected sales and agreed seasonal patterns across Chai Me’s recurring product range.
  3. Prepare a proposed monthly production plan showing products, quantities, preferred production days and due dates.
  4. Translate the approved production plan into raw-material, ingredient and packaging requirements, then flag shortages, lead-time risks and data conflicts.
  5. Present the production and procurement recommendations for human approval before creating or changing records in operational systems.
  6. Track forecast versus actual production so planning rules improve using verified Chai Me data rather than assumptions.
2

Prospect research, personalisation and HubSpot outreach

  1. Start from an approved prospect list or compliant data source and research each business using permitted public information and supported integrations.
  2. Compare the prospect’s venue, product range and positioning with Chai Me’s approved B2B proposition.
  3. Prepare a concise, prospect-specific outreach draft and recommended offer without inventing facts, pricing or authority.
  4. Create or update the company, contact, source and outreach status in HubSpot while preventing duplicates.
  5. Hold outbound messages for Chai Me’s approval and apply Australian Spam Act, consent, identification and unsubscribe requirements.
  6. Use a dedicated, properly configured outbound-mail setup with controlled ramp-up, reputation monitoring and volume limits so Chai Me’s primary domain is not put at risk.
  7. Capture replies, create follow-up tasks and report prospecting volume, response and conversion outcomes.

The outcome

Invescasa gets one managed operating layer that clears repetitive inbox work, captures valuable property intelligence and moves qualified opportunities through research, due diligence, reporting and approval without rebuilding a disconnected point solution each time.

Invescasa reports more than 600 property acquisitions nationally, over $1 billion transacted across ten years and 30% of transactions completed off-market. The first two major workflows

These are the first substantial flows to scope, test and move into production. Each will be introduced with clear approval points and a measurable exception queue.

1

Sensitive email triage and property-intelligence capture

  1. Connect the four high-volume mailboxes and nominated leadership inboxes in Google Workspace with least-privilege access.
  2. Build an Invescasa categorisation matrix covering contracts, client matters, property opportunities, document requests, simple yes/no actions, follow-ups, CRM activity and exceptions.
  3. Read and classify incoming messages, identify urgency and sensitivity, and attach the relevant client, property, agent and transaction context.
  4. Draft the recommended response or next action, route sensitive matters to the nominated person, and prevent contracts or high-risk external messages from being sent without approval.
  5. Extract agreed property fields, documents and non-public information from relevant email into a secure, structured internal database.
  6. Update HubSpot where the email changes a contact, deal, task or follow-up, while maintaining a traceable record of the source message and action taken.
  7. Launch in shadow mode, compare decisions against the current VA process, then progressively automate only the categories that meet the agreed accuracy and risk thresholds.
2

REIQ, REA and Domain property search to investment report

  1. Receive a structured client brief covering location, budget, property type, investment criteria and exclusions.
  2. Search approved REIQ, REA and Domain sources, together with Invescasa’s private database, using the agreed third-party scraping and browser-automation method.
  3. De-duplicate results, apply the brief and produce a ranked shortlist — for example, the ten strongest candidate properties.
  4. Capture the selling agent’s details and available property information, including price guide, vacancy, rental indicators and required due-diligence fields.
  5. Run the agreed qualification sequence by compliant channel. Outbound voice calls will only be enabled where the recipient has the required consent; otherwise the workflow will use approved email or a human call task.
  6. Request supporting documents from the selling agent and add the response and attachments to the property record.
  7. Populate Invescasa’s custom investment-report system through browser automation while an API is unavailable, with validation and exception checks around every submission.
  8. Generate the client-specific investment report, including approved finance assumptions and property figures.
  9. Send the completed report to the nominated Invescasa reviewer. Nothing is released to the client until a human approves it.

Controls confirmed during scoping

Scraping method, frequency, source terms, vendor charges and operational risk will be documented before production. Browser automation is practical while the internal report generator has no API, but it will be treated as an exception-managed bridge rather than an API-equivalent connection. External calls and sensitive communications remain behind consent and approval controls.

Executive summary

A practical first AI workforce for PROCADFAB

Following Daniel’s discussion with XoomAI, this proposal outlines a four-employee Lite deployment. The starting structure is two established AI Employee roles selected from the XoomAI catalogue and two custom roles designed after the relevant position descriptions, responsibilities, systems and approval boundaries are confirmed.

Start with a defined team

Four AI Employees provide a clear starting capacity while keeping the final role mix flexible until PROCADFAB completes selection.

Adapt to existing operations

The two custom roles will be mapped to agreed responsibilities and workflows rather than forcing PROCADFAB into generic job descriptions.

Controlled implementation

System access, permissions, workflow steps and human approvals are documented and tested before production release.

Managed after launch

XoomAI monitors, supports and optimises the agreed environment as workflows move from testing into daily operation.

Delivery approach

Confirm, configure, validate and launch

1. Confirm the four roles

Select the two catalogue employees and provide the responsibilities or position descriptions for the two custom employees.

2. Map workflows and authority

Define inputs, outputs, connected systems, data access, approval gates, exception handling and measures of success for each role.

3. Configure the Lite environment

Prepare the dedicated knowledge, permissions, integrations and workflows required for the agreed scope.

4. Test with controlled scenarios

Validate workflow behaviour, access controls, escalation paths and human review before any production use.

5. Launch progressively

Release the first approved workflows, monitor performance and refine the four employees using real operating feedback.

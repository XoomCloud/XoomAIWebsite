Proposed solution

A full XoomAgent™ workforce configured around Rimech's SharePoint 365 environment, Bluebeam Pro estimating process, management inboxes and project delivery workflows.

Tender qualification and estimating support

Build on Rimech's early estimating scripts with a managed workflow that reduces manual review without removing estimator judgement.

  • Monitor agreed tender sources and capture new opportunities
  • Screen tenders against Rimech's services, geography and capacity
  • Read tender packs and summarise scope, deadlines, risks and requirements
  • Prepare measurement and estimate inputs for estimator review where the source material allows

Email and management assistance

Give Brad, Brian and selected managers a practical assistant that understands the business and helps move work forward.

  • Triage inboxes and surface urgent client, tender and project items
  • Draft responses, follow-ups and internal instructions for approval
  • Turn email conversations into tasks, reminders and next actions
  • Maintain context across management communication and active work

SharePoint and document workflows

Use Rimech's existing SharePoint 365 structure as the source and destination for controlled project outputs.

  • Create folders and documents from agreed naming conventions
  • Work from approved spreadsheets, templates and project records
  • Generate summaries, reports, PDFs and estimate support files
  • Return completed drafts to the correct shared location with an audit trail

Operations and project visibility

Connect administrative workflows to Rimech's data-driven delivery model and live Rimech Management System.

  • Summarise progress, productivity, quality and outstanding actions
  • Flag exceptions, delays and missing information for managers
  • Support repeatable reporting across structural, piping, shutdown and labour delivery
  • Expand into finance, growth, HR and customer workflows as priorities emerge

Priority workflows

1

Tender intake and qualification

XoomAgent™ captures a tender, reads the available documents and drawings, compares the opportunity with Rimech's target work, then prepares a concise go/no-go brief covering scope, location, dates, compliance requirements and key risks.

2

Estimate preparation support

For approved opportunities, the workflow organises tender information, extracts quantities and requirements where reliable, creates the correct SharePoint folder structure, and prepares estimator inputs or Bluebeam-linked handoff material for human checking.

3

Email-to-action workflow

Client and project emails are triaged, summarised and converted into draft replies, tasks, due dates or escalation items. Routine administration moves faster while contractual changes and commitments remain behind a management approval gate.

4

Project document and reporting workflow

XoomAgent™ works from Rimech's agreed SharePoint templates and RMS inputs to produce consistent project summaries, progress reports, outstanding-action lists and client-ready documents in the right folder.

Rimech keeps control

XoomAgent™ prepares, organises and recommends. Estimators approve quantities and pricing; managers approve client commitments, finance decisions and changes to live work. The level of automation increases only when the process has proved reliable.

Implementation plan

1

Business and workflow audit

Run a 45-minute implementation scoping session with Brad and Brian, then map the current tender, estimating, email and SharePoint processes. Select the first two to three workflows with the strongest time saving and clearest operational return.

2

Secure cloud deployment and connections

Deploy Rimech's isolated cloud environment with managed backups, then connect the approved Microsoft 365, SharePoint and other systems required for the first workflows. Confirm Bluebeam integration options and any vendor requirements before committing to automation.

3

Configuration, guardrails and testing

Configure the full XoomAgent™ workforce, train it on Rimech's templates and working methods, establish approval points, and test each workflow with real examples alongside the people who currently perform the work.

4

Launch, train and optimise

Train Rimech's initial users, put the approved workflows into production, monitor results and meet regularly to fix issues, identify new use cases and expand automation as confidence and measurable value grow.

The recommended Lite workforce

The four specialist roles target the bottlenecks identified in the meeting. The two included Executive VAs provide the front door for delegation and everyday administrative support.

01 / Sales Representative

Keep every opportunity moving

Creates a consistent sales process around the team without pretending software can replace sales leadership or human judgement.

  • Capture and score leads using The Wink's qualification rules.
  • Prepare meeting briefs from CRM history, messages and prior activity.
  • Track response times, next steps, follow-up commitments and stalled opportunities.
  • Turn Fireflies transcripts into CRM updates, tasks, proposal briefs and follow-up sequences.
  • Draft tailored proposals and approval-ready client emails.
  • Escalate exceptions, weak pipeline movement and missed sales KPIs to Hamza.
Primary value: consistency and accountability

The sales team gets a repeatable operating system while Hamza stays involved only where his experience has the most leverage.

02 / Marketing Coordinator

Run the marketing work behind demand

Supports The Wink's Meta-led model with structured analysis, creative direction and campaign operations.

  • Review Meta performance and produce prioritised optimisation reports.
  • Develop pain-point-led campaign concepts, hooks, copy and landing-page briefs.
  • Monitor creative fatigue, lead quality and cost movement.
  • Coordinate approved campaign changes and keep a decision log.
  • Turn sales objections and call insights into new messaging and creative briefs.
  • Use external image or video platforms through supported APIs where required.
Primary value: faster learning loops

Marketing decisions are informed by live sales feedback instead of being isolated from what prospects actually say.

03 / Operations Coordinator

Connect the work across the business

Provides the cross-functional coordination Muhammad described: the AI workforce talks to itself, delegates tasks and progresses work without every instruction starting with a person.

  • Turn goals into assigned tasks across sales, marketing and finance.
  • Deliver daily priorities, overdue items and exception summaries.
  • Track owners, deadlines, dependencies and blocked work.
  • Route requests between specialist AI Employees and the appropriate human.
  • Maintain documented workflows and update them as the team improves.
  • Coordinate onboarding hand-offs after a deal is approved.
Primary value: work moves without constant chasing

The team sees what is owned, what is overdue and what needs a decision—without Hamza becoming the routing layer.

04 / Finance

Protect cash flow and financial follow-up

Adds the finance function discussed in the meeting, beginning with controlled accounts-receivable support rather than high-risk financial automation.

  • Monitor agreed finance inboxes and accounting-system signals.
  • Produce daily overdue and collection-priority summaries.
  • Draft polite, context-aware payment follow-ups for approved sending.
  • Track promises to pay, disputes and invoices requiring human action.
  • Escalate aged debt and exceptions before they disappear into admin.
  • Keep payments, credits and financial commitments behind approval gates.
Primary value: stronger collection discipline

Finance follow-up becomes consistent without giving the system authority to move money or make commercial concessions.

Two Executive VAs are included

The Executive VAs act as the everyday interface for Hamza and a nominated team leader. They can receive a direction such as “prepare the proposal from today's meeting”, pull the transcript, delegate the work to the relevant AI Employees, consolidate the result and return it for approval. They also cover general office administration, reminders, briefings and coordination without consuming one of the four specialist positions.

Priority workflows

1

Lead-to-meeting discipline

New leads are captured, scored and assigned. The Sales Representative confirms follow-up, prepares the team for the call and escalates any lead that is not progressing within the agreed service level.

2

Meeting-to-proposal

Fireflies provides the transcript. The Sales Representative extracts requirements, produces the proposal brief, updates the CRM and coordinates an approval-ready proposal and email—without the team rebuilding the context manually.

3

Follow-up and pipeline accountability

Open opportunities are checked for next actions, unresponsive prospects and missed commitments. The Operations Coordinator issues a clear daily exception list and pushes the appropriate follow-up work to Sales.

4

Sales-informed Meta optimisation

Sales objections, lead quality and call outcomes flow back to Marketing. The Marketing Coordinator turns those signals into campaign recommendations, new hooks, creative briefs and funnel improvements.

5

Finance follow-up

Overdue invoices, promises to pay and exceptions are surfaced consistently. Draft communications and priorities are prepared while financial approvals remain with The Wink.

Knowledge, Claude and model routing

Context

Bring the existing brain

During implementation, XoomAI will map The Wink's approved Obsidian and Markdown knowledge into the employee knowledge structure so the new workforce starts with relevant business context.

Connection

Claude integration path

The technical session with Raza will confirm whether The Wink's existing Claude environment is connected through a supported isolated account connection, approved knowledge synchronisation, or a combination of both.

Efficiency

Task-based routing

XoomAgent™ routes work to an appropriate model for the task. High-reasoning work can use a stronger model, while routine monitoring and administration use a more efficient option to avoid waste.

Usage boundary

The managed fee covers normal business use of the configured AI Employees. External video or image generation, separate third-party subscriptions, paid media and unusually intensive custom compute remain outside the included service unless agreed in writing. The system will not be configured as an unrestricted general-purpose compute environment.

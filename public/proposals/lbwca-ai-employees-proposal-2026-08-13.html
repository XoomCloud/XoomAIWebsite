Our understanding

From broad pillars to specific roles

In our follow-up meeting, LBWCA asked for a clearer way to map automation to its people, systems and day-to-day work. The AI Employee model gives LBWCA eight defined roles, each able to support multiple approved workflows. The roles can be selected from the existing library or designed around an LBWCA position description where a standard option is not the right fit.

Role clarity

Each AI Employee has a defined position description, capabilities and example workflows, making ownership and internal pairing easier.

Workflow fit

Workflows are mapped to the most appropriate AI Employee and tailored to LBWCA’s operating processes, controls and applications.

Phased adoption

LBWCA can prioritise the highest-impact workflows first, while staff begin using the available AI Employees for supported everyday work.

Proposed solution

Eight AI Employees, configured for LBWCA

LBWCA may choose any eight roles from the Employee Selector. If the available roles do not fully match the business, XoomAI can create custom AI Employees from LBWCA-supplied position descriptions as part of the eight-employee package.

Employee selection

Choose up to eight roles across the full selector, based on the teams and functions LBWCA wants to augment first.

Custom role design

Use an LBWCA position description to define a custom AI Employee where a standard role does not provide the right fit.

Workflow configuration

Map approved workflows, tools, information sources and handoffs beneath the relevant AI Employee.

Managed improvement

Continue adding and refining workflows within the eight AI Employees through the managed monthly service.

Delivery approach

From selection to useful capacity

1. Select and define the eight employees

LBWCA completes the selector and supplies any position descriptions needed for custom roles.

2. Configure the base environment

XoomAI prepares the environment and selected employees, allowing approximately two weeks for base deployment.

3. Connect approved applications

LBWCA provides authorised access through the agreed credential process. Each integration is validated for access, security and technical feasibility.

4. Prioritise and implement workflows

LBWCA ranks candidate workflows by impact. XoomAI targets an initial three to four workflows in the first month, subject to complexity and access readiness.

Executive summary

BR Legal does not need four disconnected bots

BR Legal needs one coordinated service layer that can meet clients in their preferred language, capture accurate information, keep matters moving across two offices and reduce repetitive administration—without blurring the boundary between assistance and legal advice.

Multicultural by design

BR Legal serves clients across 13 languages and more than 35 countries of origin. Hazaragi and Dari are therefore operating requirements, not optional translation features.

Two offices, one experience

Prospect and Dandenong should share consistent intake, booking, correspondence and matter-coordination standards while routing work to the right people.

Broad legal coverage

Nine listed practice areas create varied enquiries, documents, deadlines and handoffs. The AI team must adapt by matter type while preserving lawyer review.

Our recommendation in one sentence

Start with the client-facing language and intake experience that creates the greatest competitive advantage, then expand into paralegal, matter-coordination and office workflows only after each stage passes BR Legal’s acceptance tests.

Proposed AI workforce

Four roles, working as one team

Each AI Employee is a defined operational role capable of multiple approved workflows. They share structured context and hand work to people or each other, rather than creating four separate sources of truth.

Multilingual Legal Receptionist

  • Answer approved inbound enquiries and after-hours calls
  • Support English, Dari and validated Hazaragi conversations
  • Capture names, contact details, matter type and urgency
  • Book consultations across authorised lawyer calendars
  • Route emergencies, vulnerable clients and uncertain cases to people
  • Issue approved confirmations and information requests

Legal Intake & Paralegal Assistant

  • Run structured intake by practice area
  • Prepare first-pass matter summaries and chronologies
  • Extract parties, dates, obligations and key clauses from documents
  • Draft standard correspondence and engagement materials for review
  • Prepare research inputs without presenting them as legal advice
  • Escalate conflicts, uncertainty and sensitive facts to a lawyer

Matter & Compliance Coordinator

  • Track court dates, limitations, milestones and outstanding items
  • Coordinate conflict-check inputs and surface possible matches
  • Maintain KYC, engagement and matter-opening checklists
  • Route correspondence and documents to the correct matter
  • Notify the responsible fee-earner of exceptions and overdue work
  • Maintain an attributable audit trail of actions and approvals

Office & Marketing Coordinator

  • Coordinate general enquiries and internal administrative queues
  • Prepare approved educational and social content
  • Schedule content only after the required review
  • Support event, appointment and community communication
  • Prepare service reports and recurring workflow summaries
  • Keep office tasks visible across Prospect and Dandenong
The critical differentiator

Hazaragi performance must be proven, not promised

BR Legal has already tested another solution and found that spoken Hazaragi was not reliably understood. We propose making real-world language acceptance a formal gate before any client-facing launch.

01BR Legal supplies representative phrases and scenarios
02XoomAI configures vocabulary, prompts and routing
03Native speakers test telephone-quality conversations
04Failures are logged, refined and retested
05BR Legal approves release or retains human handling

Test what matters

Informal speech, regional variation, names, dates, addresses, legal vocabulary, interruptions, emotional callers and code-switching between Hazaragi, Dari and English.

Make uncertainty visible

The agent confirms critical facts, records confidence and escalates when meaning is unclear. It does not quietly guess or manufacture a translation.

Keep a safe fallback

Until BR Legal accepts the language experience, Hazaragi interactions can be captured for human callback or routed directly to an authorised team member.

Example client journey

From first call to a lawyer-ready intake

1. An enquiry arrives

A prospective client calls, submits a form or emails BR Legal. XoomAgent identifies the preferred language and obtains consent to continue through the approved intake path.

2. The receptionist captures the essentials

The agent records contact information, matter category, key parties, relevant dates, location, urgency and the client’s preferred office and communication channel.

3. Risk and conflict gates are applied

Urgent or vulnerable-client indicators trigger immediate human escalation. Party details are prepared for a conflict check; any possible match is surfaced for authorised review.

4. The right consultation is coordinated

Subject to BR Legal’s booking rules, the agent offers an appropriate time, updates the calendar and sends approved confirmation and document instructions.

5. A clean brief reaches the lawyer

The responsible team member receives the client’s verified facts, open questions, supplied documents, language preference, risk flags and agreed next step—linked to the correct record.

6. Every consequential decision remains human

A qualified BR Legal professional decides whether to act, gives legal advice, confirms conflicts and approves engagement, filings, representations and final correspondence.

Legal safeguards

Designed to assist the practice—not practise law

Human approval by design

Legal advice, conflict clearance, matter acceptance, substantive correspondence, court or authority filings, commercial commitments and legal conclusions remain with authorised BR Legal staff.

Least-privilege access

Each role receives only the system access and data needed for its approved tasks. Access is validated before deployment and can be withdrawn centrally.

Traceable work

Inputs, generated drafts, actions, handoffs, exceptions and approvals are logged so the firm can review what occurred and who authorised the next step.

Controlled knowledge

Workflows use BR Legal-approved scripts, templates, policies and source materials. Unclear, missing or conflicting instructions fail to a review queue.

Proposed technical posture

A dedicated, isolated environment; Australian data residency; no training of public models on BR Legal client or matter data; permissioned access; and action-level audit logging. Final architecture, vendors, retention and privacy controls will be documented and approved before production use.

Delivery plan

Progressive release with evidence at every gate

Phase 1 — Discovery and controls

Map call types, practice areas, scripts, language needs, calendars, LEAP access, decision rights, privacy requirements, escalation rules and baseline service measures.

Phase 2 — Dedicated environment and prototype

Configure the four roles, approved knowledge, permissions, queues and audit controls. Build the receptionist and language test workflow first.

Phase 3 — Hazaragi acceptance testing

Run scripted and unscripted calls with BR Legal’s nominated speakers. Refine pronunciation, vocabulary, clarification and fallback behaviour until BR Legal approves the result.

Phase 4 — Controlled pilot

Release a limited enquiry and booking pathway with human oversight, daily exception review and agreed operating limits.

Phase 5 — Legal and office workflows

Add paralegal, matter-coordination and approved marketing workflows progressively. Each workflow has its own owner, acceptance criteria and release decision.

Phase 6 — Managed optimisation

Review accuracy, escalation, completion, adoption and client experience. Tune approved workflows and prioritise new work through an agreed change process.

Questions Best may reasonably ask

Commercial and delivery objections, answered directly

“Why pay more than the $800 proposal?”

Do not pay more for a longer feature list. Pay more only if the live language test, legal controls, integration proof and managed-service accountability are materially stronger. This proposal makes those points testable.

“Can you guarantee Hazaragi?”

Not responsibly before testing BR Legal’s actual callers and scenarios. We will build and test the experience, expose limitations and obtain BR Legal’s acceptance before allowing it to handle Hazaragi calls autonomously.

“Will it work with LEAP?”

We will validate the available access method and each intended action. Where a reliable integration is available, it can be configured; where it is not, we will define a controlled handoff rather than promise an unsupported workflow.

“Will the AI give legal advice?”

No. It supports intake, preparation, coordination and controlled drafting. Qualified BR Legal professionals retain legal judgement, conflict clearance, matter acceptance, final review and advice.

“Are we locked into a long contract?”

The proposed service structure is month-to-month. Final commercial terms will be documented in the approved service agreement before commencement.

“How quickly can we launch?”

A base environment may be prepared quickly once access and scope are ready, but client-facing release depends on integration and language acceptance testing. We will agree a realistic plan after discovery instead of trading safety for an arbitrary date.

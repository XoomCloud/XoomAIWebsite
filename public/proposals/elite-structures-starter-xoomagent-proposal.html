The Starter Workforce

Four focused sections, configured around how Elite Structures already works across Microsoft 365, Planner, GoHighLevel, Fireflies, Xero and its social channels.

01 / Orchestrator

The operational control tower

The Orchestrator sits above the other sections, turning incoming information into priorities and showing Damien and Serenity only what needs a decision.

  • Deliver morning priorities and end-of-day summaries across leads, quotes, invoices and overdue follow-ups.
  • Route simple sales enquiries to Serenity and technical or custom-dome matters to Damien.
  • Turn Fireflies call notes, emails and GoHighLevel activity into assigned tasks with owners and due dates.
  • Escalate stalled quotes, unanswered supplier requests, approval bottlenecks and exceptions before they become delays.
Indicative time released: 2–4 hours per week

Less time checking systems, chasing status and deciding what needs attention next.

02 / Sales

Move leads from enquiry to qualified quote faster

The Sales section reduces the workload between a new enquiry and a quote ready for human review, while protecting Elite Structures from wasting time on poorly qualified opportunities.

  • Capture website, email, Google and Meta enquiries into one GoHighLevel and Planner workflow.
  • Acknowledge each lead immediately and send Serenity an SMS summary with the contact details and job requirement.
  • Qualify prospects around location, structure, installation, approvals, engineering expectations, budget fit and project timing.
  • Extract tasks from recorded calls, research regional suppliers, request live pricing and chase outstanding responses.
  • Compile supplier options and quote inputs for review, then progress to draft Xero quotes once the workflow is proven.
  • Run polite, low-pressure follow-ups so good opportunities do not go cold.
Indicative time released: 6–10 hours per week

Faster response, less supplier research and quote chasing, and more of Damien's time reserved for technical decisions and high-value opportunities.

03 / Media

Make the turnkey value visible

The Media section builds the profile Damien described: honest, useful and project-focused content that shows why Elite Structures is easier to engage than a supplier-only competitor.

  • Create a practical content plan for LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, with LinkedIn weighted towards mining and construction decision-makers.
  • Turn completed projects, lessons, common questions and technical insights into platform-specific posts.
  • Explain the real difference between engineered Australian solutions and cheaper alternatives without empty claims or competitor attacks.
  • Reinforce the turnkey promise: design, supply, installation, civils, containers and project management under one accountable team.
  • Start with approval-first drafts, then increase automation only after the tone and quality are consistently right.
Indicative time released: 4–6 hours per week

A consistent market presence without Damien or Serenity having to plan, write, resize and schedule every post themselves.

04 / Automations

Remove repeat admin, with guardrails

The Automations section connects the systems and executes the repetitive steps behind sales, quoting and finance while leaving sensitive actions in human hands.

  • Scan agreed mailboxes for supplier bills and invoices so items are not lost across Damien, Serenity and admin inboxes.
  • Cross-check suppliers against Xero or an approved supplier list, create draft bills only, and flag anything unfamiliar.
  • Move processed invoice emails into a clear folder so the team can see what has and has not been entered.
  • Connect GoHighLevel, Fireflies, Planner, Microsoft 365, Xero and the social publishing workflow.
  • Maintain an expanding regional supplier knowledge base as Elite Structures delivers work across Australia.
  • Keep approval points for quotes, payments, technical assumptions and public content.
Indicative time released: 3–5 hours per week

Fewer missed bills, less double handling and a clearer audit trail without allowing the system to approve payments or unverified suppliers.

Priority workflows

1

Lead capture and rapid hand-off

A new enquiry is captured into GoHighLevel and Planner, acknowledged immediately, summarised and sent to Serenity by SMS. Serenity handles standard enquiries and brings Damien in only when the requirement is technical, custom or commercially significant.

2

Call-to-task and lead qualification

Fireflies records agreed GoHighLevel calls, extracts requirements and creates the next actions. The Sales section continues qualification around approvals, engineering, scope, budget and timing so the team can distinguish a genuine opportunity from a price-only enquiry.

3

Supplier research and quote preparation

The system checks Elite Structures' preferred vendors first. Where a project is outside the current supplier footprint, it researches local options, requests current pricing, follows up and consolidates the responses into a quote pack for Damien and Serenity to review.

4

Invoice-to-Xero draft

Supplier bills are detected across approved inboxes, checked against known vendors and entered into Xero as drafts. New, unusual or high-risk items are held for review; nothing is approved or paid automatically.

5

Turnkey content engine

Project material and business knowledge are converted into useful posts that show how Elite Structures removes the coordination headache: one team, one purchase order, one mobilisation and one accountable path from design to handover.

Human control stays in the right places

Damien retains technical and commercial judgement. Serenity retains approval control over workflow outputs. Quotes, payments, new suppliers and public posts begin with review gates, and automation expands only after the team is comfortable with the quality.

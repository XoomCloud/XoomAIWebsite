MEG client journey

Coverage across the complete loan process

1. Attract and warm leads

Coordinate approved social campaigns, capture enquiries, segment interest, nurture prospects and invite suitable leads to book an assessment call.

2. Pre-qualification

Ask the defined questions covering transaction type, property, requested loan, value and other assets; capture every answer accurately; and prepare the file for Nick’s viability review.

3. Application and fee gate

Send the application form, initial document list and approved fee instructions. Follow up by email, SMS or call and hold progression until the required engagement confirmation is recorded.

4. Collect and classify documents

Chase Tier 1 documents needed for offer preparation and Tier 2 documents needed for lender submission and settlement. Track each item, version, verification status and exception.

5. Prepare and sign the offer

Assemble borrower, entity, loan and security information into a controlled Funding Proposal or Letter of Offer draft. After MEG approval, place it into the agreed signing workflow and follow up until completed.

6. Select and place with a lender

Shortlist lenders using the captured matrix—loan size, security type, LVR basis, geography, term, valuation requirements, turnaround and deal breakers—then prepare the briefing pack for Nick’s approval and lender submission.

7. Coordinate to settlement

Cross-reference MEG’s checklist with the lender lawyer’s requirements, chase the borrower and relevant professionals, monitor signatures and settlement actions, and maintain a visible exception list until completion.

8. Close and retain the record

Confirm approved completion outcomes, store the final correspondence and documents, update the deal record and preserve the audit history for authorised MEG staff.

Lender panel development

Daily lender outreach becomes structured intelligence

The Sales Representative will work from MEG’s Lender Introduction Call Script and capture answers directly into a controlled lender register. It will never guess an unanswered parameter or disclose client-identifying information before the required authority is in place.

Core appetite capture

Minimum, maximum and preferred loan size; land, residential, construction and commercial appetite; maximum LVR and basis; geography; security position; term and interest treatment.

Valuation and QS rules

Reassignment acceptance, eligible valuers, report age, reliance form, PI requirements, valuation basis, inspection type and QS reassignment requirements.

Placement intelligence

Funding source, credit process, pricing indications, turnaround, panel requirements, co-lending appetite, deal breakers and preferred live-deal format.

Relationship follow-up

Record every call outcome, do-not-contact status, requested material, CEO-call opportunity and follow-up date, then prepare the approved same-day email.

Delivery approach

Build the dedicated MEG environment in controlled phases

1. Monday process and authority workshop

Confirm the authoritative process map, qualification rules, approved scripts, data fields, human decision points, communications policy and first success measures.

2. Configure MEG’s four-role environment

Build the dedicated knowledge, client and lender records, workflow states, templates, permissions, approval gates and exception queues.

3. Connect MEG’s chosen applications

Integrate the agreed CRM or database, email, SMS, telephony, booking, document storage, electronic signing and social channels, subject to authorised access and technical validation.

4. Validate the loan-operations workflows

Test intake, application, document gates, controlled offer preparation, lender matrix matching, briefing reports and settlement checklists using approved non-production scenarios.

5. Launch and tune the channel mix

Release workflows progressively, review call/SMS/email usage and response data, refine scripts and routing, and expand only after acceptance criteria are met.

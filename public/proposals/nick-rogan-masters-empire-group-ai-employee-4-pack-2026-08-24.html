Delivery approach

From workflow mapping to managed operation

1. Confirm priorities and owners

Agree the first workflows, source systems, business owners and measurable acceptance criteria for each AI Employee.

2. Configure the managed environment

Prepare the four roles, access boundaries, knowledge sources and human approval points.

3. Connect and validate

Connect only authorised systems, then test data access, routing, exception handling and audit visibility.

4. Launch in phases

Release approved workflows progressively, monitor performance and refine them through the managed service.

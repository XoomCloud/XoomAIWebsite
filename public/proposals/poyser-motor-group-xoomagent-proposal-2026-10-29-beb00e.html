01Context

What we heard from Poyser Motor Group

From our session on 21 October with Chris, Brett and Linton, and from the Sales Signing Guide Chris shared afterwards.

  • Sales paperwork is manual, document by document

    Paperwork comes out of the dealer management system inside a virtual desktop, goes into PandaDoc against your templates, then each signed copy is downloaded individually, renamed to your format and filed to the deal pack folder by hand.

  • It is rarely right the first time

    Chris described a lot of manual auditing because salespeople seldom get every document right first time, and they are out on the yard with customers, not paid to be administrators.

  • One missing document stops stock control

    If a document is not in the deal pack, the stock control team handling registrations and finalising has to stop and chase it. By the time the salesperson gets to it and calls the customer, half a day is lost.

  • Senior managers want a view, not a hunt

    Chris wants to run a report on any salesperson's files and see straight away where the gaps are, with each deal checked against the standard rather than found later.

  • The same pattern runs beyond sales

    Parts, service and administration have the same filing problem. Brett also raised holdback and bonus remittances that are split across dozens of vehicles by hand in the bank reconciliation, and Chris raised voice to text for mechanics' notes.

  • Leadership needs to see it working

    Chris and Brett are the first users and want results they can show the wider leadership group, with a business case that may also remove tools you are doubling up on.

03Delivery

Go-live order

Every role is deployed into your live systems, not a sandbox. The order below puts sales paperwork first because it is the workflow you asked about, and sets out what each later stage waits on. Around two weeks from approval to a working, integrated system, subject to access.

  1. Days 1 to 7

    Your environment and Slack workspace

    We set up your dedicated Australian server and your Slack workspace, load your Sales Signing Guide and policies as the reference for every workflow, and set permissions for each role.

  2. Days 8 to 10

    Sales paperwork live

    Sales Paperwork Coordinator and Deal Pack Auditor go live on PandaDoc and your deal pack folders. Until dealer management system access is confirmed, the salesperson saves the export into one controlled folder as they do today and the workflow takes it from there. Nothing bypasses the virtual desktop.

  3. Week 2

    Reporting, reminders and Executive VAs

    Sales Administration Officer starts the daily status report and the stock control notices once real deal packs are flowing, and the Executive VAs for Chris and Brett go live on Microsoft 365. Implementation meeting and team training follow.

  4. After access is confirmed

    Dealer management system, accounts and service

    Direct export from the dealer management system waits on the API documentation and access from your provider. Remittance allocation and service notes follow once the access and the first workflows are agreed with Brett and the service team.

Composite case study

More capacity. Without another hire.

How a growing Australian multi-site services business could reclaim 38 hours a week by turning disconnected admin into one managed workflow.

This is an illustrative composite based on common Australian SMB workflows. The organisation and figures are modelled examples, not claims about a named XoomAI client.

The situation

Growth had outpaced the back office

Three locations, 28 staff and a steady flow of enquiries. The team was capable, but work was being slowed by repetitive hand-offs between inboxes, spreadsheets, a CRM and accounting software.

01

Enquiries stalled

New leads waited hours for acknowledgement while staff checked shared inboxes between other tasks.

02

Data was re-keyed

Customer details moved manually between email, CRM, job management and reporting sheets.

03

Visibility arrived late

Managers spent Friday afternoons assembling reports that were already out of date.

The managed workflow

One XoomAgent™ connected the work

XoomAI mapped the process, set clear approval points and configured a managed AI workforce around the systems already in use.

2. UnderstandClassify intent, urgency and missing information.

4. UpdateCreate records and keep CRM stages current.
5. ReportDeliver a live daily summary to management.

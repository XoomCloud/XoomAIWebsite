Information entered on this website

The enquiry form prepares an email draft on your device. It does not submit your details to a website database or automatically send a message. You choose whether to send the draft using your email application. The copy action copies your prepared enquiry to your device's clipboard.

When you contact XoomAI

If you send an email or use XoomAI's booking page, your name, contact details and enquiry are shared with XoomAI so the team can respond. The email or booking service you use also processes information under its own terms. Please avoid including passwords, sensitive personal information or confidential client documents in an initial enquiry.

Cookies, media and hosting

This website code uses no advertising trackers, analytics cookies or browser storage. Artwork, fonts and the optional film are served as local website assets. A hosting provider may process request metadata such as IP addresses and server logs. Hosting arrangements and a full business privacy policy must be confirmed before public launch.
